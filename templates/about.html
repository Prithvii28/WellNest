THE IDEA BEHIND WELLNEST

A little more aware.
A little more you.

WellNest is a student-built personal wellness journal for noticing your own everyday patterns.

01

Made for reflection

Log weight, sleep, water and movement when you choose. Browse your entries and see summaries of your own records.

02

Built with Python

The project uses Flask, Python functions, dictionaries, SQLite, Pandas and responsive charts.

ⓘ

Not medical advice

WellNest does not diagnose, treat or recommend health decisions. BMI is a limited screening measure, not a diagnosis or complete measure of health.
